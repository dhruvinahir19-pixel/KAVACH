# STAGE 4 — Full Backtest 2025 → 2026: Regime Test of the Morning Reveal Protocol

**Question asked:** *"Backtest our setup from 2025 to 2026 current. Ensure that all results are real, no logic mismatch, no look-ahead bias, no error."* The motivation: rejecting shorts on the Apr–Sep 2026 window alone could be regime bias — both sides must be tested across 18 months with regime segmentation.

**One-line answer:** You were right. The short side is **not** broken — the Apr–Sep 2026 window was a coincidental stretch that flattered longs and hid shorts. Over 18 out-of-sample months **both sides are profitable**, and in calendar 2025 **shorts were the better side**. Shorts stay in the system.

---

## 1. Test setup (identical rules to Stage 3, extended window)

- **Selection:** evening Top-10 mover picks, walk-forward retrained monthly (no leakage — first OOS month Apr 2025, so every trade below is scored by a model that never saw that month).
- **LONG:** pick with 9:45 price > opening-range (9:15–9:30) high → long at 9:45 close, exit 15:10 close.
- **SHORT:** pick with 9:45 price < OR low → short at 9:45 close, exit 15:10 close.
- **Costs:** 0.15% round trip on every trade.
- **OOS period:** 2025-04-01 → 2026-09-08 (18 months, 355 mornings, 3,522 pick-days).

Walk-forward engine health over the same window: mean hit 52.7% (base 20.2%), mean AUC 0.709, capture 1.48×, monthly hit 42.9–63.6% — the selection layer held up across both years.

## 3. Regime segmentation

**Descriptive split** (full-month Nifty return / VIX average — explains what happened, but the month return contains future info relative to trade dates):

| Nifty UP month | **64.3% / +0.77%** | 57.8% / +0.16% |
| Nifty DOWN month | 53.6% / +0.20% | **59.4% / +0.50%** |
| VIX high (≥12.8) | **62.4% / +0.68%** | 55.9% / +0.09% |
| VIX low (<12.8) | 55.8% / +0.30% | **61.5% / +0.58%** |
| Nifty down + VIX high | 46.7% / +0.03% | 50.7% / −0.05% |

**Tradeable split** (regime known at trade time: *prior* month's Nifty return / VIX):

| prior-month regime | LONG win / avg | SHORT win / avg |
|---|---|---|
| Nifty up | 59.1% / +0.45% | 58.7% / +0.21% |
| Nifty down | 59.0% / +0.55% | 58.4% / +0.46% |
| VIX ≥ 12.9 | **61.1% / +0.63%** | 56.1% / +0.12% |
| VIX < 12.9 | 56.8% / +0.33% | **61.1% / +0.53%** |

Findings:
1. **Both sides are profitable in every tradeable regime split.** No regime in which a side must be switched off — except the descriptive crash-chop corner (Nifty down + VIX high), where *both* sides go flat (~50%, ~0%). That corner is only 11% of pick-days and is identified with future info here; a real-time version would need an intramonth trailing regime check.
2. **The Nifty-trend tilt mostly washes out** when you only use prior-month information — the up-month/down-month contrast above is largely coincident-month descriptive, not exploitable.
3. **The VIX tilt survives** the tradeable test: after high-VIX months longs do best (+0.63%), after low-VIX months shorts do best (+0.53%). This is a *candidate* tilt rule, fit on this sample — treat as hypothesis for forward testing, not a validated rule.

## 4. Controls & risk

- **Same-day control (18 months):** all OR-up stocks 46.4% up / −0.10% avg; **picks** OR-up 61.9% / +0.60%. The pick×breakout interaction edge holds across both years (it was 72.6% vs 45.2% in the 2026-only window — the full-window separation is smaller but robust).
- **Streaks:** max consecutive losing trades = 8 on both sides. Plan psychology/sizing for it.
- **Worst single trades:** long −11.07%, short −9.71%. Best: long +10.41%, short +18.80%.
- **Day clustering (1× each side):** 346 of 355 mornings traded; avg +1.50%/day; 57% win-days.
  - **Worst day −21.39% (2025-12-09):** six correlated longs (AMBER −5.25, PGEL −6.89, GODREJPROP −4.56, KALYANKJIL −2.60, BSE −1.89, COFORGE −0.19) on a gap-down tape. This is the real risk of the protocol: same-day picks are correlated; a equal-weight book can lose ~20% in one session at full 1× size.
  - Best day +31.15% (2025-08-08).
- **Combined book 1× long / 0.5× short (as currently specified):** avg **+1.18%/day**, 60% win-days, worst day −15.25%, total **+407pp over 18 months**.
- **Coverage:** median 10/10 picks per morning; 16 mornings had <10 (universe/data joins early 2025); PEL delisted 2025-07-31 marginally reduces 2025 coverage; GUJGASLTD/IDFC died pre-window.

## 6. Verdict

1. **Shorts stay, at 0.5× size.** 702 OOS trades, 58.5% win, +0.32% net avg, best side of calendar 2025, best side of low-VIX regimes. Dropping them on the 2026 window would have been exactly the regime bias you suspected.
2. **Longs stay at 1×.** 601 trades, 59.1% win, +0.49% net avg — real but weaker than the first six-month look suggested.
3. **VIX tilt is the one candidate regime rule** (favor longs after high-VIX months, shorts after low-VIX) — carry it into forward testing as an *observation*, not a rule, until it survives live paper data.
4. **Risk framing for sizing:** expect 8-trade losing streaks, −11% single trades, and −15 to −21% equal-weight days when everything correlates. Position size must be set so a 2025-12-09-type day is survivable.
